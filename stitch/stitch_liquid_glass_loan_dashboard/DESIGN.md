## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the discretion, precision, and sovereign confidence of bespoke institutional lending. The brand evokes an uncompromising atmosphere of security, calm authority, and high-tier liquidity management. Geared toward ultra-high-net-worth individuals, institutional principals, and family offices, the interface prioritizes absolute clarity over ornamental decoration.

The visual style is rooted in **Architectural Dark Minimalism**:
- Heavy reliance on intentional whitespace (or deep dark "air") to project luxury and composure.
- Purely monochromatic values that refuse neon or saturated visual cues.
- Surface transitions driven by delicate tonal tiering rather than harsh delineations.
- Data-rich views rendered effortless through strict typographic hierarchy, generous padding, and razor-sharp typographic alignment.

## Colors

The palette is strictly zero-saturation monochrome, relying on nuanced tonal step-downs to create depth and affordance. 

### Surface Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#08090b`):** The foundational pitch-black background for maximum depth and contrast.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#0d0f12`):** Primary panel and shell container background, seamless against the base.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#14171d`):** Card structures, distinct data blocks, and elevated modules.
- **Surface Level 3 (`#1c2027`):** Interactive resting states, table row hover backgrounds, nested wells.
- **Surface Level 4 (`#262b35`):** Active states, input backgrounds, and micro-segment chips.
- **Subtle Surface Line (`#3a414e`):** Reserved exclusively for structural dividers and hairline indicators where tonal layering alone cannot preserve semantic clarity. Never used as heavy decorative framing.

### Typographic & Element Contrast
- **Crisp Light (`#f8fafc`):** Primary reading layer, large-scale financial metrics, and high-impact calls to action.
- **Cool Silver (`#94a3b8`):** Secondary metadata, column labels, descriptions, and non-active icons.
- **Subdued Slate (`#64748b`):** Tertiary timestamps, unit markers, and disabled states.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized across the entire platform to maintain clean geometric rhythm, architectural precision, and uncompromising readability.

- **Numerals & Data:** Financial tables, balances, and loan interest rates require `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to maintain pristine vertical baseline and column tracking.
- **Labels:** Micro-labels and table headings leverage uppercase tracking via `label-sm` or `label-md` with `0.04em` to `0.06em` letter spacing for fast optical scanning without drawing undue focus.
- **Weights:** Keep structural weights strictly between `400` (for context and explanation) and `500` / `600` (for data markers, values, and headlines). Avoid ultra-bold weights (`700`+) to preserve a lightweight, refined presence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a rigorous, spacious 12-column grid system designed to let financial positions breathe and reduce cognitive friction.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` gutters, `3rem` screen margins. Maximum container width capped at `1440px` for ultra-wide displays to preserve legibility.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` gutters, `2rem` screen margins. Dual-pane views collapse into hierarchical stacked modules.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` screen margins. Full horizontal utilization with generous vertical padding to prevent touch overcrowding.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Prioritize high negative space (`space-xl` and higher) around key decision blocks, portfolio valuations, and transaction sign-offs.
- Internal component distances stick strictly to an 8pt/4pt sub-rhythm (`space-xs` through `space-lg`).
- Avoid cluttering data panels: stack metrics vertically with disciplined spacing rather than packing adjacent blocks into cramped boxes.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated purely through **tonal stacking** and subtle, diffused ambient occlusion. Hard lines, saturated glows, and heavy outlines are explicitly avoided.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pitch-black `#08090b`. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Sub-structures):** `#0d0f12`. Sits flat on the canvas; separation is defined by background tint differential.
- **Level 2 (Cards & Modules):** `#14171d`. Ambient drop: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Level 3 (Flyouts, Menus & Dropdowns):** `#1c2027`. Elevated drop: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Level 4 (Modal Dialogs & Critical Approvals):** `#262b35`. Dominant focus drop: `box-shadow: 0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`, combined with a dimmed canvas backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background-color: rgba(8, 9, 11, 0.8)`).
- **Hairlines:** When distinct division is mandatory within data-heavy tables or split panes, apply a single-pixel separator using `#3a414e` at 40% to 60% opacity (`rgba(58, 65, 78, 0.5)`).

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses **Soft (`1`)** corner curves. This subtle radius imparts modern engineering elegance while retaining architectural seriousness and mathematical precision.

- **Micro elements (Tags, Checkboxes, Micro-inputs):** `0.25rem` (`4px`).
- **Standard Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** `0.25rem` (`4px`) to `0.375rem` (`6px`).
- **Cards & Data Modules:** `0.5rem` (`8px`).
- **Major Overlay Sheets & Modals:** `0.75rem` (`12px`).
- **Pill overrides:** Avoid fully rounded pills. Buttons, tags, and chips must consistently honor the linear architecture with soft corner geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Crisp white background (`#f8fafc`) with deep pitch-black text (`#08090b`), font-weight `600`. Hover shifts subtly to `#e2e8f0`. Active state scales smoothly to `0.985`.
- **Secondary:** Tonal slate container (`#1c2027`), text in `#f8fafc`. Hover lifts background to `#262b35`. Zero exterior borders.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Transparent background, text in `#94a3b8`. Hover transitions to text `#f8fafc` and an ephemeral background of `#14171d`.
- **Padding & Sizing:** Standard button height is `44px` (`space-sm` vertical, `space-lg` horizontal) to satisfy both touch targets and aesthetic balance.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Container:** Dark charcoal base (`#14171d`), height `44px`, text in `#f8fafc`.
- **Placeholder:** `#64748b`.
- **Focus State:** No bright focus rings. Transition container background to `#1c2027` with a delicate outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #3a414e`.
- **Labeling:** Position labels above inputs in `label-sm` with `#94a3b8`.

### Cards & Financial Modules
- **Construction:** Rendered in `#14171d` against the `#08090b` canvas. Padding must be spacious: at minimum `space-lg` (`1.5rem`), expanding to `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) on hero summary cards.
- **Data Display:** Prominent metrics leverage `metric-numeral` or `display-lg` in `#f8fafc`, nested with descriptive micro-labels below or above in `#94a3b8`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default:** `#14171d` background, text `#94a3b8`, 4px radius, `0.375rem` vertical and `0.75rem` horizontal padding.
- **Selected:** Background ascends to `#262b35`, text transitions to `#f8fafc`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Unchecked:** Solid `#1c2027` fill, 4px corner radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. No outline.
- **Checked:** Solid `#f8fafc` fill with the tick or core indicator rendered in `#08090b`.

### Lists & Institutional Data Tables
- **Rows:** Alternating rows rely on pure rhythm rather than harsh striping. Default background transparent; hover reveals `#0d0f12` or `#14171d`.
- **Separators:** Bottom borders use hairline `rgba(58, 65, 78, 0.4)`.
- **Alignment:** Numbers, loan amounts, and percentages right-align; counterparties and statuses left-align.
- **Table Headers:** `label-md` tracking, `#94a3b8`, uppercase.

### Bespoke Lending Components
- **LTV / Collateral Health Trackers:** Segmented tonal bar indicators using `#1c2027` for track capacity and `#f8fafc` for deployed drawdowns. No traffic-light greens or reds; utilize pure white intensity and micro-callout labels (`#94a3b8`) for threshold warnings.
- **Vault Access Verification:** Dedicated credential lockup with monospace key representations set against `#0d0f12` wells with `#64748b` token strings.
